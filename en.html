Areas of practice

Our expertise

We put our expertise at your service to address your employment, social protection and social security law matters.

Employment contracts

Drafting, amending and terminating employment contracts. Support at every stage of the contractual relationship.

  • Mutual termination agreement
  • Employment contracts
  • Disciplinary procedures
  • Disciplinary sanctions

Dismissal

Support throughout dismissal procedures, whatever the reason.

  • Redundancy on economic grounds
  • Dismissal for misconduct
  • Dismissal on grounds of incapacity
  • Collective procedures (PSE, RCC, etc.)
  • French Labour Court

Occupational health

Support on workplace health and safety matters.

  • Workplace accident
  • Occupational disease
  • Psychosocial risks (burnout, workplace stress, etc.)
  • Disability

Workplace harassment & discrimination

Support in workplace harassment and discrimination situations.

  • Workplace harassment
  • Workplace discrimination
  • Burnout
  • Work-related distress

Business advice

Legal support for businesses and senior executives in managing employment matters.

  • Social protection
  • Employment law compliance (audit)
  • Collective agreement
  • Collective bargaining and agreements
  • CSE (Social and Economic Committee): ongoing support, professional elections and challenges to expert assessments
  • Industrial action and strikes
  • Restructuring

Social security / URSSAF

Expertise in social security law and disputes involving the General Social Security Fund (CGSS).

  • URSSAF disputes
  • Social contributions
  • URSSAF inspections
  • Challenging workplace accident / occupational disease decisions
  • CGSS litigation
