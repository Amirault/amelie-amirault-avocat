Our expertise

Employment contract

Drafting, amendment and termination of employment contracts. Support throughout the employment relationship.

  • Disciplinary procedure
  • Disciplinary sanctions

Dismissal

Support through dismissal procedures, whatever the reason.

  • Redundancy
  • Dismissal for misconduct
  • Dismissal for incapacity
  • Collective procedures (PSE, RCC...)

Occupational health

Support with health and safety at work issues.
