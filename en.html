About us

Legal support grounded in attentive listening, rigour and practical solutions.

AMIRAULT AVOCATS is a law firm dedicated to defending your rights in employment, social and social security law.

Founded by Ms Amélie Amirault, a lawyer registered with the Martinique Bar, the firm advises employees, executives, directors and companies throughout their professional lives.

Our approach is based on careful listening to your needs, an in-depth analysis of your situation and the implementation of tailored strategies to best defend your interests.
