Dismissal

Preparing, conducting or challenging dismissal proceedings with method, rigour and clear visibility on the risks.

Anticipating every stage of the procedure

We intervene early to assess the situation, secure discussions and define an appropriate strategy. In a dispute, we assist you in negotiations and before the French Labour Court.

Our services include

  • Assessing the grounds and evidence
  • Preparing invitations, meetings and dismissal letters
  • Supporting economic dismissals and restructurings
  • Defending a dismissal challenge
  • Negotiating a settlement or exit agreement
